# Marilyna's — the pizza-ingredient recipe gap

_Generated by `ops/gen_mari_gap.py`. Quantified only; no recipes invented._

## What this is

Marilyna's has no till of its own — its sales are an attributed slice of the
Stowaway till, and its stock is **Stowaway's**, ordered against Stowaway par
SKUs. Its packaged drinks now flow through to those pars automatically.

Its **pizzas do not**, because a pizza is a finished good: it consumes flour,
mozzarella, pepperoni, bases and boxes through a RECIPE. Without recipes that
consumption is invisible, so the pars for those inputs carry no demand signal
at all — they are set by hand and cannot self-correct.

## The size of it

- **215 Marilyna's POS lines** sell with no recipe
- **660 units/week** (8,574 over 13 weeks)
- **$136,281 ex-GST** of sales over 13 weeks

## What this means for the pars today

Checked honestly, only **one** pizza input currently carries a live par:

| live par | SKU | model says |
|---:|---|---:|
| 22 | Halal Pepperoni Slice Fettayleh [1kg] | 22.0 |

Every other pizza input (mozzarella, pizza flour, dough, tomato sauce, the
boxes) sits at par 0 — i.e. Marilyna's stock is currently managed OUTSIDE the
par system entirely, by hand.

So the recipe gap is not, today, causing bad pars on those items: there are no
pars on those items to be bad. What it does mean is that the model **cannot be
switched on** for Marilyna's stock until the recipes exist — and that the one
input that IS parred (pepperoni) is running with no demand signal behind it.

## Consequence

Until these recipes exist, Marilyna's is a large, invisible draw on Stowaway
stock. Pizza-input pars will keep needing manual correction, and the shrinkage
engine will misread the gap as loss rather than unmodelled consumption — the
one place a shrinkage number should be treated with suspicion.
